AI video · from the insideNeural synthesis
Family A

The model imagines pixels.

Its gift

Atmosphere, weather, crowds, cinematic filler, cameras that could not physically fly. Fast to a gorgeous first look.

Hand it to another machine

Exact words. Exact numbers. A mechanism you could check. A product that must match itself in shot twelve and shot forty.

There is no object inside the model. There is a learned sense of how scenes like this tend to look and move.

AI video · from the insideRendered scenes
Family B

Code computes the pixels.

You describe

Shapes, text, meshes, lights, a camera, keyframes over time.

It calculates

Each frame is a function of the scene at that time. Repeatable.

Agents thrive

The scene is a program they can write, run, read, and patch.

You pay in build time. You get exact type, exact data, and a vehicle that cannot redesign itself between shots.

A video is a function of time.

for frame in frames:
t = frame / fps
image = render(scene.at(t))
write(image)
encode(frames, audio)
The picture at a frame depends only on that frame number, and on inputs you froze. Wall-clock timers flicker, because frames render out of order and in parallel.

Remotion centres on the current frame. HyperFrames seeks the timeline to the frame it is about to capture.

AI video · from the insideAssembly

It places

Which asset, when, what size, which transition, which audio underneath. The timeline is data.

Three early wrecks.

1

One model, whole film

Clips are short. Diagrams belong to the renderer. Neural shots are atmosphere.

2

It watches like you

Often a few frames, plus text. Video models commonly sample about one frame a second.

3

Audio as paint

Audio is the clock. Pictures are cut to the words, not the other way around.

TOMATO temporal-reasoning benchmark, as cited in the field guide: people about 95%, best model under 40%. Flicker and one-frame errors live in that gap.

AI video · from the insideThe agent

What the evidence supports.

Decomposition beats a crowd

Published evidence that many specialist agents beat one well-grounded agent is thin. A planner that turns the job into shots is the part that keeps showing up.

The critic is mostly probes

Loudness. Speech-to-script diff. OCR on titles. Black frames and freezes. A vision model aimed at cuts and cue words. Taste stays at the human gate.

Everything you can turn into a number should be a number.

AI video · from the insideRouting and reality

Three routing rules.

Exact

Text, data, a mechanism, a logo. Render it.

Plausible

Beautiful is enough. Generate it.

Both

Render the exact layer. Generate the plate. Or restyle a render you control.

A recurring character is an asset, not a prompt. Narration is its own track when the words must be exact.

AI video · from the insideRouting and reality
A map, not legal advice

Disclose it. Sign the final export.

Article 50

EU transparency duties for covered synthetic media have applied since 2 August 2026. Say what was generated. Have a person confirm the duty for your release.

Provenance

If you sign a manifest, sign the final export. An FFmpeg re-encode strips it. A signature on an intermediate file signs the wrong artefact.

Keep a register beside the shot list: asset, licence, voice consent, likeness. The agent maintains it. You decide what goes in.
